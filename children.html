Who We Are

Our children's ministry offers a safe, joyful place where little ones learn Bible stories, sing songs of praise and discover how much Jesus loves them.

  • Children's Sabbath School with age-appropriate lessons
  • Bible stories, memory verses and songs
  • Creative activities, drama and crafts
  • Special children's programs and celebrations
  • Caring volunteers who partner with parents
