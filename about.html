About Our Church

A community of worship, hope, love and spiritual growth in Kigalama.

SDA Kigalama Church is a Christian worship community committed to sharing the love of Christ, studying the Bible and encouraging spiritual growth.

Our Mission

To share the gospel of Jesus Christ and help people prepare for His second coming.

Our Vision

To build a loving church community that serves God and supports others.

Our Values

Faith, love, prayer, service, unity and obedience to God's Word.

“I lift up my eyes to the mountains—where does my help come from?” — Psalm 121:1
